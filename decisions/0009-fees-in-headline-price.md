# Booking fees are included in every headline price

- **Status:** accepted (owner delegated to Claude's recommendation)
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Owner:** repo owner (@Orlando80)
- **Applies to:** `.claude/skills/policy-uk-refunds` rule UKR-6

## Question
How are booking and other mandatory fees shown?

## Options
1. Included in the headline price wherever a price first appears; optional extras labelled separately **(chosen)**
2. Shown as a separate line from the first price display
3. Added at checkout

## Why
Required by the DMCC Act 2024 drip-pricing rules since 6 April 2025; adding fees at checkout is unlawful.

## Consequences
Prices are computed and displayed as totals; the fee breakdown is visible but never added later.
