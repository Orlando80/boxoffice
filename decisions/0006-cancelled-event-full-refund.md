# Cancelled events refund everything, automatically, within 14 days

- **Status:** accepted (owner delegated to Claude's recommendation)
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Owner:** repo owner (@Orlando80)
- **Applies to:** `.claude/skills/policy-uk-refunds` rule UKR-3

## Question
When an event is cancelled, what is refunded, how and how fast?

## Options
1. Everything paid (face value, booking and delivery fees), automatically to the original payment method, within 14 days **(chosen)**
2. Face value only; booking fees kept (STAR permits this if disclosed)
3. Credit or vouchers instead of cash

## Why
Matches the CMA's position under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and needs no customer action; keeping fees or forcing credit invites enforcement and complaints.

## Consequences
Cancellation triggers a refund workflow for every order; no claim form; credit may be offered only alongside cash.
