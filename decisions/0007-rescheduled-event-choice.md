# Rescheduled or materially changed events: keep or refund, 14 days to choose

- **Status:** accepted (owner delegated to Claude's recommendation)
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Owner:** repo owner (@Orlando80)
- **Applies to:** `.claude/skills/policy-uk-refunds` rule UKR-4

## Question
What happens to tickets when an event moves date, venue or headliner, or changes significantly?

## Options
1. Customer chooses keep or full refund, at least 14 days to decide; no reply means keep **(chosen)**
2. Tickets move automatically; refunds only on request
3. Automatic refund of everyone

## Why
Follows CMA guidance (refund if the customer can't attend or the event is significantly different) while keeping sales for those who can still go.

## Consequences
Material change = new date, venue or headline act, or a seat downgrade. Each affected order gets a keep-or-refund choice with a deadline.
