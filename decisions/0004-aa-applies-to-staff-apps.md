# WCAG 2.2 AA also applies to admin and scanner

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Owner:** repo owner (@Orlando80)
- **Applies to:** `.claude/skills/policy-accessibility` rule A11Y-1

## Question
Does the WCAG 2.2 AA bar cover staff-facing apps, or only customer-facing surfaces (the brief says customer-facing)?

## Options
1. Customer-facing surfaces plus admin and scanner **(chosen)**
2. Customer-facing surfaces only

## Why
Box-office and door staff can be disabled too, and venues owe their staff reasonable adjustments.

## Consequences
Specs for admin and scanner features apply the same rules; any gap is flagged for the owner.
